# The language

An interface in linen is paper, hairlines and one ink. The content is the loudest thing on a page; the chrome around it steps back. `src/styles/linen.css` holds every token below, and `src/motion/tokens.ts` every duration. A component that states a colour, type size, radius or duration instead of naming a token is wrong; spacing comes from the 4px scale. The specimen (`pnpm specimen`) draws all of it with the components themselves.

## Colour

Every neutral takes the app's `--hue` at a chroma of 0.02 or less, so the page is one temperature. Tints are mixes of an ink into the surface, never colours picked by eye. Each colour is one `light-dark()` pair; the page follows the system scheme, and `data-theme` on the root pins either.

| Token                                       | Marks                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ink`, `accent`                             | What a hand can act on or has set: the primary action, links, focus, the current place, a chosen option |
| `accent-weak`                               | The ink mixed into the surface: hover, the current place's fill, a notice                               |
| `warn`                                      | A state that wants attention, and nothing else                                                          |
| `danger`                                    | Removal and failure                                                                                     |
| `positive`                                  | Something gained or arrived                                                                             |
| `positive-weak`, `warn-weak`, `danger-weak` | Each state's ink mixed into the surface, as `accent-weak` is: the ground of a message in that state     |
| `bg`, `surface`, `sunken`                   | The page, a card on it, a band below the page (the rail, a well, a form opened inside a card)           |
| `border`, `border-strong`                   | Hairlines between surfaces; the frame of a control                                                      |
| `text`, `text-secondary`, `text-muted`      | The thing; what it means; text about it (labels, units, times)                                          |

Depth is tone. Nothing at rest casts a shadow; `shadow-overlay` belongs to what floats over the page: popups, dialogs, toasts and the rail's tips.

## Type

| Voice   | Face             | Speaks for                                                 |
| ------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Display | `--font-display` | The wordmark and a screen's title                          |
| Working | `--font-sans`    | Everything else                                            |
| Mono    | `--font-mono`    | What is typed or copied exactly: addresses, commands, keys |

Hierarchy comes from weight and colour before size: a step down in importance moves from `text` to `text-secondary` to `text-muted` first. Everything is in sentence case. Figures read in columns are tabular.

## Layout

| Part       | Component                                    | Holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shell      | `Shell`                                      | The rail on a desk (foldable to its icons) and the screen beside it. Below `md`, `phoneNav` puts the places in a tab bar along the bottom (the default) or in a bar along the top that scrolls away. The wordmark, and the mark of a folded rail, lead `home`. The rail's head holds a choice under the wordmark, such as what the places belong to, and its foot the account menu and choices beside it (`RailChoice`); a folded rail's choice opens under its name. A phone's bar holds the places alone, so the app offers what the head and foot hold on a screen as well, such as its settings |
| Top bar    | `TopBar`, `TitleField`                       | The screen's title at the left, its actions at the right; sticky. A screen under another names the screens above it in a small `trail` over the title, each a link; past two steps the outer ones fold behind one button, so the trail stays one short line. An editable name is a `TitleField`, as wide as its words. `wide` spans an editor's whole width                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Sections   | `SectionNav`                                 | A screen's concerns as routes, under the top bar; one row that scrolls on a phone                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Column     | `Content`, `COLUMN_CLASS`                    | Every band centres its contents in `--width-content`, so a title and the card below share edges                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Card       | `Card`, `CardHead`, `CardBody`, `FormBand`   | One concern each. The head holds a title, a count beside it, a description and actions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Setting    | `SettingRow`, `SettingToggle`, `SettingNote` | One setting per row: name, what it does, its control at the end of the row while it fits beside the words and below them once it does not                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Empty      | `EmptyState`                                 | What would be here, and the way to start                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Conditions | `Conditions`                                 | What holds for the whole app, such as lost contact or a run under way: a band at the top of every screen, stuck above its bars until the app takes it away. Two or more stand one under another, a failure first, then a warning, then what is under way or simply so, then what came good                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

Anything that floats at the foot of a phone's screen, as a toast does, stands `--inset-tabbar` above the edge: the tab bar's height while one is on the page, nothing otherwise. The frame starts below `--inset-top`, the band a translucent status bar covers in an app saved to a phone's home screen: the rail and the phone's top bar reach up under it in their own colour, and everything sticky sticks below it. A screen outside the shell keeps clear of it itself.

Every card's contents start on `--spacing-gutter` (12px, 16px from `md`). Two settings never share a line. Actions sit beside a card's title while there is room and under its description once there is not.

## Controls

| Height token               | Height | Used by                                      |
| -------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| `--height-control`         | 32px   | Buttons, fields, pickers, segmented controls |
| `--height-control-compact` | 28px   | Filters and bar chips                        |
| `--height-control-inline`  | 24px   | Inline edits and row chips                   |

- A button wears a frame and a fill, never words alone. One primary per card, form or dialog. A destructive button sets its words in `danger` inside the same frame and opens a confirmation. A link that starts an action wears `buttonClass()`; a link in running text wears `LINK_CLASS`.
- A field wears the same frame everywhere, its name above it and its hint below. A unit sits inside the frame, after the value.
- A picker ends in one chevron, which turns while its list is open. Its list is never wider than the screen beside it, and a long choice wraps. A list of more than one kind of thing holds each kind under its heading. A field's stated width is its control's, and gives way to a narrower row. A narrow field's name and messages may be wider than its control: they take the room the field stands in, its cell in a grid or column, and in a row of fields the field widens to hold them, so a refusal reads in a line. A row of fields keeps its controls on one line, and a message hangs below it.
- A setting that takes effect as it turns is a `Switch`; a choice a form submits later is a `Checkbox`. A choice whose answers each need a line is `OptionCards`.
- One value out of a few answers of a word or two, which all fit the row, is a `SegmentedControl`. More answers, or longer ones, are a `Select`, and a list nobody can scan is a `SearchSelect`.
- Making something asks in a `FormDialog`; every dialog lays out its title, description, body and actions with `DialogLayout`.
- A dialog stands in the part of the window a person can see, an on-screen keyboard's room taken out, and never grows past it. In a window too short for all of it, the title and the actions keep their places and what lies between them scrolls, with a hairline at each edge something lies past, so every field stays within reach.
- A dialog's confirm that starts something taking a moment returns its promise, and the dialog waits until it settles, kept or failed: the confirm is busy, and neither Cancel, Escape nor a press beside it leaves. The app bounds the wait, so an answer that never comes ends as a failure the dialog says. No dialog closes itself on confirm; the app closes it once the thing is done, or keeps it open with the error that says why not.
- A destructive act confirms in a `ConfirmDialog` that says what it does, an alert that a press beside it does not dismiss. An act that can be put back exactly takes a toast's undo instead.
- Work a person keeps with a save button is a draft: `useUnsavedChanges` reports it to the one `UnsavedChangesGuard` around the pages, which holds the app's moves through its navigation's `useBlocker` and asks before leaving a page that holds unsaved drafts, names them, and lets the browser ask before the tab closes. A setting that takes effect as it turns is no draft.
- A toast is Base UI's: it stands over every dialog and answers a press without closing it. F6 takes the keyboard to it and Shift+Tab back, its time rests while a pointer or the keyboard is on it, and a swipe or Escape sends it away.
- A control that starts something asynchronous is `busy` until the answer comes: a pass of light crosses its face, it keeps its words and its width, and it takes no second press. A switch that writes as it turns holds the same way.
- A field whose value was refused says why under the control, before its hint, in `danger`; the control is marked invalid and described by both.
- A refused submit takes the focus to the first field it refused, each time it is refused, so its name and its error are read together. A `Form`, and with it every form dialog, does this once the answer has come: at once, or when the promise its submit returned settles. A submit before then starts nothing. A field's own check (`validate`) refuses before the app is asked; the app's answer refuses through the form's `errors`, by each field's `name`. The app sets `errors` from every answer, nothing once nothing was refused, and never clears them as a submit starts. Each error stands until its value is edited, and the form refuses a submit at once until then, so a field's error says only what is wrong with its value; a refusal for any other reason is a `Notice`.
- The state something is in is a `Status`: a dot in the tone and a word or two. Words that want attention, a warning or a failure, wear the ink too, and something happening now beats. A state the person cannot change, such as access that only reads, is an `ExplainedStatus`, which says why in its `description`: its words, underlined in dots, open it under a resting pointer, at a press or at a key, and a press or a key takes the focus to it, so it is read out.
- A failure, warning, note or success about an act or some fields is a `Notice` beside them, on its tone's tint, with the one thing to do about it at its end. A failure is read out at once; the others are read politely when their words change while they stand.
- A value on a bounded scale is a `Gauge`: a track, the scale, with its zones in a line under it; a fill, the value, in the ink of each zone it reaches; marks where the value was held or a threshold is set; and the value in words. Only the fill is tinted, so a gauge at its lowest reads as empty. Gauges read together stand in `Gauges`. In a row of a list, gauges are `compact`: their bars alone, thin, with no line of zones, their names and values read out but not shown. A value that changes many times a second is handed over as something it follows, so nothing around it renders.
- A value to take whole is a `CopyValue`; a secret shown once is a `ShownOnce`, which says what losing it means in the app's words where the default does not fit. Names and values read at a glance are `Facts`: each name starts its line and its value ends it, on the edge a setting's control stands on, a hairline between one fact and the next, and a value too long to stand beside its name goes under it. A value typed exactly is set in mono and taken whole.
- A number set within a range is a `NumberField`: it moves in steps, ten at a time with Shift or Page Up and Page Down, and to either end with Home and End, its unit inside the frame after it.
- Something under way in place is a `Progress`: its phase in words over a bar that fills, or, where it cannot tell how far, a stretch that travels along the bar.
- A task of known steps shows them with `Steps`: each done, the one under way and the ones to come. Back, Cancel and the next step are its buttons, and it may open at any step, the ones before it done.
- Text a machine writes is a `LogView`: monospace in a bounded box that follows new lines until the reader scrolls up, copied or saved whole.

## Motion

Motion says what changed and where it went, and never holds input.

- A change applies at once and the animation follows it. Anything leaving is inert from its first frame.
- Every frame is true: an interrupted animation turns from where it stands.
- Only opacity and transforms move, except a height that is itself the change (a panel unfolding, a view swapping).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` makes every duration zero and leaves loops at rest, read live.
- Motion moves between states and never holds one. A state a person reads, such as a tip, the direction of a fold, a mark, a fill or a turn, is the stylesheet's, so an app without Motion keeps every state and every motion CSS runs.

Durations are springs with no bounce; a component names a purpose, never a length. `motion.css` holds the first three as `--motion-leave`, `--motion-state` and `--motion-enter`, and the holds and loops as `--motion-mark`, `--motion-land`, `--motion-thinking` and `--motion-live`; a test holds them to `src/motion/tokens.ts`.

| Token      | Length | For                                                                                              |
| ---------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `leave`    | 120ms  | Something on its way out                                                                         |
| `state`    | 180ms  | A mark sliding, a control changing, a panel folding                                              |
| `enter`    | 280ms  | An arrival, or travel to a new place                                                             |
| `stagger`  | 80ms   | The step between related arrivals                                                                |
| `mark`     | 1200ms | A new row's tint fading                                                                          |
| `land`     | 1000ms | A refresh indicator's turn                                                                       |
| `thinking` | 1500ms | One pass of light across a control waiting on an answer, or along a bar that cannot tell how far |
| `live`     | 2000ms | One beat of something happening now                                                              |

| Pattern                | Component                                             | Motion                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A screen changes       | `Shell`                                               | The next screen fades in over `enter` once it has drawn                                                                                                                |
| A popup opens          | `POPUP_CLASS`                                         | Grows from its control's edge over `enter`, leaves toward it over `leave`                                                                                              |
| A dialog opens         | `DialogRoot`, `DialogShell`                           | Rises in the middle with its scrim over `enter`, keeps its words while it leaves                                                                                       |
| A panel unfolds        | `DisclosurePanel`                                     | Travels to its height over `enter`, folds over `state`                                                                                                                 |
| A view swaps           | `Swap`                                                | Travels between heights over `enter` while the views cross-fade                                                                                                        |
| A choice moves         | `SegmentedControl`, `SectionNav`, the phone's top bar | The chosen mark slides over `state`, beneath the options it passes; in the top bar the places make room over `state`, then the chosen one's words fade in over `enter` |
| A toast comes and goes | `ToastProvider`, `useToast`                           | Arrives over `enter`, fades over `leave`; follows a swipe and leaves the way it was swiped                                                                             |
| A list changes         | `SettlingRows`                                        | Rows close up over `enter`; an arrival wears `accent-weak` for `mark`. A list takes its `scope` once its rows have loaded, so none of them reads as an arrival         |
| A control waits        | `Button` and `Switch` with `busy`                     | One pass of light across its face each `thinking`; still, the light rests across its middle                                                                            |
| Something is live      | `Status` with `active`                                | A ring leaves its dot and fades each `live`; still, the ring stays drawn                                                                                               |
| Something runs         | A condition with `active`                             | Its mark turns once each `land`                                                                                                                                        |
| How far is not known   | `Progress` without a value                            | A stretch travels along the bar each `thinking`; still, the bar fills faintly                                                                                          |
| Something arrives      | `arrive()`                                            | Settles once with a 4px rise over `enter`, `stagger` apart                                                                                                             |
| The theme changes      | `createThemeStore`                                    | One cross-fade of the whole page                                                                                                                                       |

Typing, hovering, focusing, sorting and filtering stay instant: they are the reader's own acts, repeated many times a minute.

### The frame budget

At 120 Hz a frame has 8.3ms. An animation the compositor runs is smooth whatever the main thread does; one that needs the main thread is smooth only while nothing else runs there.

- An animation never re-renders React on each frame. A component that observes its own size observes only what it lays out.
- What costs a render happens before the motion starts: a screen change draws the next screen while the old one is still shown, and only then fades it in.
- Layout projection (Motion's `layout` and `layoutId`) moves small things: a chosen mark, the places of the phone's top bar and the rows of a list. Scroll containers declare `layoutScroll`, so scrolling is not mistaken for a row edit, and a new list scope remounts its rows without travel or arrival marks.
- Reduced motion also turns off row and shared-element projection, and the theme changes without its cross-fade.

## One mechanism for each job

A component does each of these jobs the one way linen does it, so a new component reads like the old ones and a change to a mechanism reaches all of them.

| Job                                                                         | Mechanism                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What a widget does for the hand and the keyboard: focus, keys, dismissal    | Base UI's part for it, which linen styles and never re-implements                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| A control's name, what describes it, and why its value was refused          | Base UI's field, which every field and setting's row stands on and every Base UI control inside reads; an option of a group takes `aria-labelledby` on its own words, which win over the field's name the group takes, and a box with its words is named by them the same way; a control with no name in sight takes `aria-label`                             |
| A Base UI part opening and closing                                          | CSS in `src/styles/motion.css`, on Base UI's `data-starting-style` and `data-ending-style` and its springs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| A state that shows or turns: a press, a tip, a fold, a fill, a mark, a loop | CSS in `src/styles/motion.css`, on the same springs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Movement between states: a mark sliding, rows settling, an arrival          | Motion, timed by `src/motion/tokens.ts`: `spring()`, `seconds()`, `arrive()`; without Motion, it lands where it would have ended                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| A link, and which place is current                                          | The app's `navigation`, handed to `LinenProvider`: its `Link` renders every link, and the current place, the address itself or one beneath it, wears `aria-current="page"`. No component imports a router                                                                                                                                                     |
| A component's classes and the caller's `className`                          | `classes(own, className)`, so the caller's word on a property wins; an app joins its own classes with it too                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Words a person reads                                                        | `useWords()`, which an app may translate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| A component's props                                                         | A value it holds is `value` and `onValueChange`, a state that is on or off `checked` and `onCheckedChange`, and what opens `open` and `onOpenChange`. What it is called is `title` and what it means `description`, both any content. A part that wraps one native element takes that element's props, and every part that stands in a flow takes `className` |

## Every action shows its end state

A control answers rest, hover, focus, active and disabled in its own body, and busy wherever it starts something asynchronous. A write lands somewhere visible, a failure names what failed and offers the retry where the fault is, and a state a person can enter but cannot see or leave is a defect.
